🎯 25 Golden Exam One-Liners for DSSSB Reasoning Ability Must Revise 2 Times

  1. Opposite Letter Pairs: Sum of positions of opposite letters from A to Z is always 27 (e.g. A(1) + Z(26) = 27; K(11) + P(16) = 27).
  2. EJOTY Formula: E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25. Reverse backward position = 27 - forward position.
  3. Morning Shadow Rule: Sun in East $\implies$ Shadow in West. If your shadow is to your left, you are facing North.
  4. Evening Shadow Rule: Sun in West $\implies$ Shadow in East. If your shadow is to your left, you are facing South.
  5. Noon Shadow Rule: At exactly 12:00 Noon, sun is directly overhead; zero shadow is cast on the ground.
  6. Position Interchange: Total persons = (Person A's New Rank) + (Person B's Old Rank) - 1.
  7. Overlapping in Row: If Left + Right > Total, persons between them = (Left + Right) - Total - 2.
  8. Circular Arrangement Facing Inside: Clockwise movement = Left turn; Anti-clockwise movement = Right turn.
  9. Circular Arrangement Facing Outside: Clockwise movement = Right turn; Anti-clockwise movement = Left turn.
  10. 'Only a Few A are B': Definitely means BOTH 'Some A are B' AND 'Some A are NOT B'. 'All A being B' is IMPOSSIBLE.
  11. 'Only A is B': Reverses to 'All B are A', and B can NEVER touch or overlap with any other entity.
  12. Cube Painted Corners: Exactly 3 faces painted are ALWAYS 8 cubes at the 8 vertices, regardless of n.
  13. Cube Painted Edges: Exactly 2 faces painted = 12 · (n - 2).
  14. Cube Painted Surfaces: Exactly 1 face painted = 6 · (n - 2)².
  15. Unpainted Core Cubes: 0 faces painted = (n - 2)³.
  16. Standard Dice: Sum of opposite faces is ALWAYS 7 (1 opposite 6, 2 opposite 5, 3 opposite 4).
  17. Single Face Common Dice Rule: Rotate clockwise from the common number in both positions to find opposite pairs.
  18. Clock Angle Formula: Angle $ heta = |30H - rac{11}{2}M|$ degrees.
  19. Clock Hands Coincidence (0°): Hands coincide 22 times in 24 hours (11:00 to 1:00 has only 1 overlap at 12:00).
  20. Clock Hands Opposite (180°): Hands are straight opposite 22 times in 24 hours (5:00 to 7:00 has only 1 opposite at 6:00).
  21. Clock Hands Right Angle (90°): Hands form a 90° angle 44 times in 24 hours.
  22. Leap Year Repetition: A leap year calendar repeats after 28 years. An ordinary year repeats after 6 or 11 years.
  23. Odd Days in Centuries: 100 years = 5 odd days; 200 years = 3 odd days; 300 years = 1 odd day; 400 years = 0 odd days.
  24. Blood Relations Nephew/Niece: Nephew is sibling's son; Niece is sibling's daughter.
  25. Pythagorean Triplet: Distance between (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) = $\sqrt{\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2}$. Classic triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17.
