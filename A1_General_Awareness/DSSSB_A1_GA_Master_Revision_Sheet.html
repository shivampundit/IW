🎯 30 Golden Exam One-Liners for DSSSB General Awareness Must Revise 2 Times

  1. Article 239AA(4): Caps the Delhi Council of Ministers at 10% of the total 70 assembly seats (maximum 7 ministers including CM).
  2. Delhi Legislative Restrictions: Delhi Assembly cannot legislate on State List Entry 1 (Public Order), Entry 2 (Police), and Entry 18 (Land).
  3. Article 32: Supreme Court writ jurisdiction (called 'Heart and Soul of the Constitution' by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar).
  4. Article 226: High Court writ jurisdiction (broader than SC as it applies to legal rights beyond fundamental rights).
  5. 86th Constitutional Amendment 2002: Inserted Article 21A (Right to Free Education 6-14 yrs) and added 11th Fundamental Duty 51A(k).
  6. 61st Constitutional Amendment 1988: Lowered voting age in Lok Sabha and Legislative Assemblies from 21 to 18 years.
  7. 42nd Amendment 1976: 'Mini-Constitution'; added 'Socialist', 'Secular', and 'Integrity' to the Preamble; added Part IVA Fundamental Duties.
  8. 44th Amendment 1978: Removed Right to Property from Fundamental Rights (Art. 31) and made it a legal right under Article 300A.
  9. Third Buddhist Council: Held at Pataliputra (250 BCE) under the patronage of Emperor Ashoka; compiled Abhidhamma Pitaka.
  10. Poorna Swaraj Resolution: Passed in December 1929 Lahore INC session presided by Jawaharlal Nehru on the banks of river Ravi.
  11. Alauddin Khilji's Market Reforms: Established Diwan-i-Riyasat and Shahna-i-Mandi; introduced Dagh and Huliya systems.
  12. Mahatma Jyotirao Phule: Founded Satya Shodhak Samaj in 1873 in Pune and authored 'Gulamgiri'.
  13. Hilton Young Commission: Recommended the establishment of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI Act 1934, commenced 1 April 1935).
  14. M3 (Broad Money): Equals M1 (Currency + Demand Deposits) + Time Deposits with the banking system.
  15. GNP vs GDP: GNP = GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA).
  16. Salivary Amylase (Ptyalin): Digestive enzyme in saliva that converts dietary starch into maltose disaccharide.
  17. Mitochondria: Synthesizes ATP through aerobic cellular respiration (Powerhouse of the Cell).
  18. Plaster of Paris (POP): Chemical formula is CaSO4 · 1/2 H2O (Calcium Sulphate Hemihydrate); made by heating gypsum at 373 K.
  19. Rainbow Formation: Involves Refraction, Dispersion, and Total Internal Reflection (TIR) of sunlight inside water droplets.
  20. Echo Minimum Distance: Requires an obstacle at least 17.2 meters away in air (based on 0.1 s persistence of hearing).
  21. Myopia (Near-sightedness): Image forms in front of retina; corrected using a Concave (diverging) lens.
  22. Hypermetropia (Far-sightedness): Image forms behind retina; corrected using a Convex (converging) lens.
  23. Lothal (Gujarat): World's first tidal dockyard and port city of the Indus Valley Civilization on river Bhogava.
  24. Kalibangan (Rajasthan): Archaeological evidence of the earliest ploughed agricultural field and fire altars.
  25. Dholavira (Kutch): UNESCO World Heritage Site with unique 3-tier city planning and sophisticated rock-cut water reservoirs.
  26. Devprayag: Confluence of rivers Alaknanda and Bhagirathi in Uttarakhand where the river takes the name Ganga.
  27. Narmada & Tapti: West-flowing peninsular rivers flowing through rift valleys; form estuaries instead of deltas.
  28. Thomas Cup & Uber Cup: World Men's and Women's Team Badminton Championships, respectively.
  29. Kathakali: Classical dance-drama of Kerala characterized by elaborate facial makeup and colorful costumes.
  30. Qutub Minar Iron Pillar: 4th-century CE Gupta Empire unrusted metallurgical marvel carrying Brahmi inscription of King Chandra.
