Distributions: Poisson Equal

Root: Newton-Raphson

x_(n+1) = x_n - f(x_n)/f'(x_n).

Simpson: 1/3 Rule Constraint

(h/3)*[(ends)+4(odds)+2(evens)].
Strictly requires n to be an EVEN number.

Trapezoidal: Integral

(h/2)*[(ends) + 2*(middle)].
Error O(h^2); RK4 solves ODEs O(h^4).
