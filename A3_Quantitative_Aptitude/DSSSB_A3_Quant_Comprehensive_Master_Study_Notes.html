Boats & Streams Master Equations
Let boat speed in still water = u km/h, stream current speed = v km/h.
• Downstream Speed (D) = u + v
• Upstream Speed (U) = u - v
• Boat in still water u = (D + U) / 2
• Stream current speed v = (D - U) / 2
• Ratio of times for same distance: t_up / t_down = (u + v) / (u - v).
